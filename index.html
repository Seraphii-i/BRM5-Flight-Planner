AVIATION TERMINOLOGY & GRID SYSTEM

BRM5 GRID COORDINATE SYSTEM

The operational map utilizes a standard Easting/Northing coordinate box system. Grid coordinates are expressed as EE XXX / NN YYY, where EE represents the primary Easting column (00-07), NN represents the primary Northing row (00-03), and XXX/YYY represent fine sub-grid positional precision scaled within that cell.

ALTITUDE & NAVIGATION TERMS

  • AGL (Above Ground Level): Altitude relative to the terrain directly beneath the airframe.
  • MSL (Mean Sea Level): Barometric altitude relative to sea level.
  • Ingress: The tactical route approach segment leading toward the objective or LZ.
  • Egress: The departure flight path away from the objective area towards safe airspace or RTB.
